# Perception LMS — Copyright Policy

**Owner:** Yoodem, Compliance and Risk Officer · **Status:** Draft · **Issue:** #101
**Related:** [terms-of-use.md](terms-of-use.md), [privacy-policy.md](privacy-policy.md), [data-retention-policy.md](data-retention-policy.md)

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets the rules for the course materials lecturers upload and the work students hand in through Perception LMS. It's based on the Australian *Copyright Act 1968*.

It's general guidance, not legal advice. Items marked **(confirm with ASA)** depend on ASA College's own licences, employment contracts and intellectual property (IP) policy.

## 2. Who owns what

| Content | Who owns the copyright | What ASA College may do with it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Student work** (assignments, quiz answers, files) | The **student** | Store it, mark it, check it for academic integrity, and keep it for appeals and records, for as long as the [retention policy](data-retention-policy.md) says. Not publish or share it outside the course without the student's permission |
| **Materials a lecturer makes as part of their job** (slides, notes, recordings) | Usually **ASA College**, because work made by an employee as part of their job belongs to the employer unless an agreement says otherwise (confirm with ASA's IP policy and employment contracts) | Use it in its courses |
| **Materials made by a contractor or guest lecturer** | The **creator**, unless they agreed in writing to transfer it | Use it only as their agreement allows. Get a written licence before uploading |
| **Third-party material** (textbook pages, articles, images, videos) | The **original author or publisher** | Use it only under a licence, a copyright exception, or with permission (section 3) |
| **Announcements and feedback** | The person who wrote them, or ASA College for staff | Show them in the course |

Uploading something to Perception LMS never transfers its copyright.

## 3. What lecturers may upload

### Always fine

- **Your own work** that you made for ASA College.
- **Material ASA College has a licence for**, such as library databases and e-books, within the licence terms. Linking to the library copy is usually safer than uploading a file.
- **Open material** under a Creative Commons or similar licence, with the credit the licence asks for.
- **Material with written permission** from the copyright owner. Keep the permission.
- **Links** to legitimate web pages and videos, such as YouTube links. A link isn't a copy.

### Fine within limits: the education licence

Australian educational institutions can copy and share text and images for teaching under the **education statutory licence**, which the Copyright Agency administers. This only applies if ASA College has an agreement with the Copyright Agency **(confirm with ASA)**. Usual limits:

- **Books:** up to **10% of the pages, or one chapter**, whichever is more
- **Journals and newspapers:** **one article** from an issue, or more if they're on the same subject
- **Images:** a whole image, if it isn't sold separately within a reasonable time and price
- **Broadcast TV and radio:** recordings under the Screenrights licence **(confirm with ASA)**

Material copied under this licence must:

1. be shared only with students enrolled in that course. Perception LMS does this, because only enrolled people can see a course
2. show this notice on the file or next to it:

   > *This material has been reproduced and communicated to you by or on behalf of ASA College in accordance with section 113P of the Copyright Act 1968 (the Act). The material in this communication may be subject to copyright under the Act. Any further reproduction or communication of this material by you may be the subject of copyright protection under the Act. Do not remove this notice.*

3. include the source: author, title, publisher and year.

### Other exceptions

Other exceptions, such as **flexible dealing for education (section 200AB)** and **fair dealing for criticism or review**, are narrow and hard to apply. Ask the Compliance and Risk Officer or the library before relying on them.

### Not allowed

- Whole books, or more than the limits above
- Pirated copies, or content downloaded from sites that don't own it
- Full films, TV series or music albums, unless ASA has a licence that covers them
- Material from a subscription service you signed up for personally, unless its terms allow teaching use
- Anything with its copyright notice, credit or watermark removed

## 4. What students may hand in

- Your own work. You keep the copyright in it.
- Short quotes and extracts from other people's work, credited properly. Fair dealing for **research or study** and for **criticism or review** usually covers this.
- Don't upload other people's whole works, such as a full textbook chapter, unless your lecturer asks for it.
- Using someone else's work without crediting it can be plagiarism, even when it's legal under copyright. ASA College's academic integrity policy applies.

## 5. Sharing course materials

Course materials are for students enrolled in that course only. Students must not upload them to other websites, such as essay-sharing or study-note sites, or share them outside the course unless the lecturer allows it. This is also in the [terms of use](terms-of-use.md).

## 6. Reporting and removing material that breaks copyright

**Anyone** can report material they think breaks copyright: a copyright owner, a student or a staff member.

1. **Report:** email **[copyright contact] (confirm with ASA)** with:
   - the course and the name of the file or page
   - what work you think is being copied, and who owns it
   - your name and contact details
2. **Check:** the Compliance and Risk Officer replies within **2 business days** and checks whether the material is allowed.
3. **Remove:** if it isn't allowed, or the check will take a while, an admin or the lecturer **unpublishes or deletes** it, usually within **2 business days** of the report. Deleting a material in Perception LMS also deletes its file from storage.
4. **Tell:** the person who uploaded it is told why it was removed. They can reply with evidence that it's allowed, such as a licence or permission, and it's put back if so.
5. **Record:** each report is logged with the date, the material, the decision and who made it.

If a student uploads infringing material, it may also be handled under ASA College's student conduct policy. If a staff member does it repeatedly, it may be handled under staff policies.

## 7. Who does what

| Role | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Lecturers | Check copyright before uploading; add the section 113P notice and sources; respond to removal requests |
| Students | Hand in their own work, credit their sources, and don't share course materials outside the course |
| Admins | Remove reported material when asked; keep the log of reports |
| Compliance and Risk Officer | Owns this policy; checks reports; advises lecturers |
| ASA College library | Advises on licences and the education statutory licence (confirm with ASA) |

## 8. Gaps in the system today

These items go to the technical team:

1. **No place for the copyright notice.** Materials have a description field, but nothing reminds lecturers to add the section 113P notice. A "copied under the education licence" tick-box that adds the notice automatically would help.
2. **No reporting button.** Users have to email to report material. A "Report a problem" link on each material would make reporting easier.
3. **No record of removals.** Deleting a material leaves no record of who deleted it or why. This is the same gap as in the retention policy and DPIA (risk P-12).
4. **No way to hide a material without deleting it**, other than unpublishing its whole module. A per-material "hide" would allow removal while a report is checked.

## 9. Review

This policy is reviewed **once a year**, when ASA's copyright licences change, or when the system starts accepting new kinds of content, such as video recordings or discussion posts.
