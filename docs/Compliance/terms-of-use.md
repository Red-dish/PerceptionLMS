## Terms of use for Perception LMS

**Last updated:** 11 November 2026

Perception LMS is ASA College's online learning system. By signing in, you agree to these terms. They sit alongside ASA College's student and staff policies, such as its academic integrity and IT acceptable use policies. If they ever disagree, ASA College's policies come first.

## 1. Who can use it

- Only people ASA College has added can sign in: enrolled students, lecturers and authorised staff.
- You must sign in with the Google account the college added for you.

## 2. Your account

- Your account is for you only. Don't share it, and don't let anyone else sign in as you.
- Sign out when you use a shared or public computer. Your sign-in lasts up to 8 hours.
- Keep your Google account secure. We recommend turning on 2-step verification in Google.
- If you think someone else has used your account, tell **ASA College IT (confirm contact with ASA)** straight away.
- You're responsible for what's done with your account, unless you reported it as misused.

## 3. Using it properly

**You may** use Perception LMS for your studies or your teaching work at ASA College.

**You must not:**

- try to see other people's work, marks or accounts, or get around any access limit
- hand in work that isn't your own, or help someone else cheat. ASA College's academic integrity policy applies
- upload anything harmful: viruses, programs, or files meant to damage the system or other people's computers
- upload anything illegal, offensive, threatening or harassing, or private information about other people without their consent
- upload material that breaks copyright. See the [copyright policy](copyright-policy.md)
- try to break, overload, scan or test the security of the system, unless the technical team has asked you to in writing
- use automated tools to download content or collect other people's details
- share course materials outside the course unless your lecturer allows it

## 4. Your work and course materials

- **Students** keep the copyright in their own work. By handing it in, you let ASA College store it, and let your lecturers and the college use it to mark and assess you, check for academic integrity, and handle appeals.
- **Lecturers' materials** are shared only for the course they're posted in. See the [copyright policy](copyright-policy.md) for who owns them and what may be uploaded.
- Keep your own copy of important work. Perception LMS keeps earlier attempts, but you should still have your own backup.

## 5. Lecturers and staff

- Only look at student information you need for your teaching or job.
- Grade exports (CSV files) contain personal information. Store them only on college systems or devices, and delete them when you no longer need them.
- Release marks only when they're final and checked.

## 6. Privacy

How we handle your personal information is explained in the [privacy policy](privacy-policy.md).

## 7. If you break these terms

ASA College may limit or turn off your account while it looks into a problem. Breaking these terms may also be handled under ASA College's student or staff conduct policies. Illegal activity may be reported to the police.

## 8. Availability

We try to keep Perception LMS running at all times, but it may sometimes be unavailable for maintenance or because of problems we can't control. If the system is down near a due date, tell your lecturer. They can give an extension.

## 9. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms. We'll change the date at the top, and tell you in Perception LMS about important changes before they take effect.

## 10. Questions

Contact **ASA College IT (confirm contact with ASA)**, or the privacy officer for privacy questions (see the [privacy policy](privacy-policy.md)).
