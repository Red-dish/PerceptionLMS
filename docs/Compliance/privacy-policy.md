## Your privacy at Perception LMS

Perception LMS is ASA College's online learning system. You use it to see course materials, hand in assignments, get marks and feedback, and read announcements.

This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, where it's kept, who can see it, and what you can do about it. "We" means ASA College, which runs Perception LMS.

**Last updated:** 11 November 2026

## 1. What we collect

| What | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Your **name** and **email address** | To let you sign in and to show your name to your lecturers |
| Your **role** (student, lecturer or admin) and the **courses** you're enrolled in | To show you the right courses and let you do the right things in them |
| **Work you hand in**: typed answers and files, including earlier attempts | So your lecturers can mark it |
| **Marks, feedback, due dates, late or missing work, and extensions** | To record your results and help lecturers support you |
| **Course materials and announcements** you post (lecturers) | To share them with your class, with your name as the author |
| **Technical records**: when you used the system, the pages you asked for, error messages, your IP address and browser type | To keep the system working and secure |

**We don't collect:** passwords, your date of birth, home address, phone number, payment details, or health information. Please don't put sensitive information, such as health details, into a submission unless your lecturer asks for it. If you need to share it, for example for an extension, follow ASA College's usual process.

## 2. How we collect it

- **From ASA College:** an admin adds your name, email and role, and enrols you in courses.
- **From Google:** when you click "Sign in with Google", Google confirms your email address. We don't get your Google password, contacts or anything else in your Google account.
- **From you:** the work you hand in and anything you type.
- **From your lecturers:** your marks and feedback.
- **Automatically:** technical records when you use the system.

## 3. How we use it

We use your information only to:

- run your courses, take your submissions, and record and release your marks
- let lecturers see who's handed in work late or not at all, so they can follow up
- keep the system secure and fix problems

We **don't** sell your information, use it for advertising, or use it for anything unrelated to your studies. If we ever want to use it in a new way, such as an AI tutor or automatic alerts about students who may need support, we'll update this policy and tell you **before** that starts. From 10 December 2026, this policy will also say if a computer program makes decisions that significantly affect you. Perception LMS doesn't do this today.

## 4. Where it's stored

- Everything in Perception LMS is stored by **Amazon Web Services (AWS) in Sydney, Australia**.
- Files are encrypted while stored, and all connections use HTTPS.
- Files you upload can't be reached by the public. Even a download link only works for **5 minutes**.

### Services outside Australia

| Service | What it gets | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Google** (mostly in the US) | The fact that you signed in to Perception LMS | To confirm who you are when you sign in |
| **Cloudflare** (worldwide network) | Your IP address and browser type when you load the website | To deliver the website quickly and protect it from attacks |

Neither service receives your coursework, marks or feedback.

## 5. Who can see it

| Who | What they can see |
| --- | --- |
| **You** | Your courses, your submissions, and your marks and feedback once your lecturer releases them |
| **Your lecturers** | For courses they teach only: the names and emails of enrolled students, their submissions, marks, feedback, and late or missing work |
| **Other students** | Nothing about you, except your name on announcements or materials if you post them |
| **ASA College admins** | All accounts, courses, enrolments, and late or missing work, to run the system |
| **The technical team** | Technical records and, only when fixing a problem, the stored data |

Students can't see other students' work or marks. If you aren't enrolled in a course, you can't see that it exists.

We'll share your information outside ASA College only if the law requires it, or if you agree.

## 6. How long we keep it

We keep information only as long as we need it for teaching, appeals and the law. For example:

- your account: while you're active, then **2 years** after it's closed
- your submitted files: **1 year** after final marks are released
- your marks and feedback: **7 years** after the course ends, then moved to ASA College's student records
- technical records: **30 days**

The full list is in the [data-retention policy](data-retention-policy.md). **(confirm periods with ASA)**

## 7. Your rights

You have the right to:

- **See your information:** ask for a copy of what we hold about you.
- **Correct it:** ask us to fix a wrong name or other details. If you think a mark is wrong, talk to your lecturer first.
- **Ask us to delete it:** we'll delete what we don't have to keep. Some records, like final marks, must be kept by law, and we'll tell you which ones and why.
- **Complain:** see section 9.

**How to ask:** email **[privacy officer's email] (confirm with ASA)** from the email address you use to sign in. We'll reply within **30 days**. It's free.

## 8. If something goes wrong

If a data breach is likely to cause you serious harm, we'll tell you and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC), as the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme requires. We'll say what happened and what you can do.

## 9. Complaints

1. Contact ASA College's privacy officer first at **[privacy officer's email] (confirm with ASA)**. We'll reply within **30 days**.
2. If you're not happy with our answer, you can complain to the **OAIC**: [oaic.gov.au](https://www.oaic.gov.au), phone **1300 363 992**.

## 10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If the change is important, for example a new use of your information, we'll tell you in Perception LMS before it takes effect.
