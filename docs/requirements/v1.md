# v1: Perception LMS MVP

**Status:** superseded by v2 on 15 Nov 2026 (the MVP features stay; v2 added AI). **Date:** 28 Oct 2026. **Source:** lecturer.

See [project-outline.md](../project-outline.md) and [user-stories-mvp.md](../Product%20Management/user-stories-mvp.md).
- **The product:** a web LMS to replace Canvas at the college, for students, lecturers and administrators.
- **Features:** Google sign-in with roles; a dashboard; course materials; assignments, submissions, grades and feedback; announcements; and admin management of users, roles and enrolments.
- **Delivery:** serverless on AWS at perceptionlms.com, in one-week cycles with a deliverable due every Wednesday.
